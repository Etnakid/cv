Rhein-Neckar Labour Market

How different are the job markets in six nearby cities? I used official German labour statistics for 2023–2025 to compare employment, unemployment and demographic gaps. The workflow connects Python data preparation and validation, PostgreSQL analysis and Power BI reporting.

KEY FINDING

Worms has the largest gender employment gap in the June 2025 comparison: 13.44 percentage points.

02 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

BASF Business Performance

Living in Ludwigshafen made me curious about BASF’s business. I analysed nine published financial indicators across 2016–2025, using Power Query to prepare the data, PostgreSQL to investigate trends and Power BI to communicate the results.

KEY FINDING

In 2025, sales fell 2.91% while free cash flow rose 79.41% compared with 2024. Published series have comparability limits.
